## Rules I'd hold to in the copy

- **No "revolutionary".** The claim is narrow and checkable: a room, a price per
  party, a clock, a frozen claim. Narrow claims survive a hostile reviewer.
- **Say the unknown out loud.** The honesty about an unstated clock is the
  differentiator, not a weakness to hide.
- **Don't promise predictions.** It is a structured hypothesis with a date on it,
  and the app says so on screen.
- **Don't tag people who didn't ask.** Tag the org, not individuals.
